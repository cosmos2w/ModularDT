# Thermal state and finite-response learning

The state-response research recipes compare the current inventory-normalized relation-anchored reader (H0), its evidence-aware role-strength capability (H1), and a separate all-native query-read control (Q0). All retain the same contextualizer, original-member nonlinear messages, prescribed Thermal inputs and joint physical-field head. H1 adds six zero-initialized scalar coefficients; historical configurations retain their parameter inventory and equations. Q0 executes contextual exchange and reads all eligible original members at each query, with one evaluation of its identical paired views.

The three explicit [H0](../../configs/research/thermal_r16_1_h0_train150.json), [H1](../../configs/research/thermal_r16_1_h1_train150.json), and [Q0](../../configs/research/thermal_r16_1_q0_train150.json) recipes start fresh with common seed-zero initialization, TRAIN150-only normalization, a constant AdamW schedule and a 1000-epoch horizon. Their native fitting population is the existing input-stratified TRAIN150 manifest. Auxiliary fitting additionally exposes all original FIT48 baseline families and their four physical edit endpoints, including families outside TRAIN150. Edited response-holdout labels never enter fitting or scale estimation. Canonical VAL89 is historically exposed state validation; the TRAIN-origin response holdout measures edited responses rather than unseen-layout generalization. Running these recipes requires an explicit authorized experiment budget and the existing external data and reference packs.

Literal membership in the maintained manifests places 15 FIT baselines inside TRAIN150 and 33 outside it; all 48 receive auxiliary baseline/endpoint supervision. The twelve response-holdout baselines split into two inside TRAIN150 (0079 and 0185) and ten outside. Under full TRAIN600 all these original TRAIN baselines receive native state supervision, while their holdout edited labels remain withheld. Report these exposure groups explicitly rather than treating an outside-TRAIN150 FIT baseline as entirely unseen.

The objective is the maintained paired full/P95 native state loss plus 0.25 times the mean absolute endpoint loss plus 0.1 times the finite-response loss. The latter combines normalized field deltas, material smooth-maximum change and inlet-minus-outlet pressure change with weights 0.8, 0.1 and 0.1. Response scales use equal-family, equal-action FIT RMS changes with TRAIN state/repeatability floors; functional definitions and their empirical resolution floors retain the source study's semantics. Complete canonical receivers determine the functionals. Each endpoint uses its own physical support; field deltas use aligned original-member/local receiver identities and common valid support. Saved observation labels remain separate from any harmonized re-extraction of saved fields.

Four FIT families and all four of their actions are visited each epoch. A shuffled family cycle covers all FIT48 families over twelve epochs. The case provider distributes these families across the native optimizer updates and scales their loss contributions so that the epoch mean is the equal family/action mean, independent of native batch count. Each action is backpropagated before the next action is prepared. The same maintained optimizer performs native and auxiliary learning; no response-only head or optimizer is added. Histories record response exposure separately from primary case visits, and paired fitting is not sparse-only execution.
