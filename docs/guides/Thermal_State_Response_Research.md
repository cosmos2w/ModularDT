Report these exposure groups explicitly rather than treating an outside-TRAIN150 FIT baseline as entirely unseen.

The objective is the maintained paired full/P95 native state loss plus 0.25 times the mean absolute endpoint loss plus 0.1 times the finite-response loss. The latter combines normalized field deltas, material smooth-maximum change and inlet-minus-outlet pressure change with weights 0.8, 0.1 and 0.1. Response scales use equal-family, equal-action FIT RMS changes with TRAIN state/repeatability floors; functional definitions and their empirical resolution floors retain the source study's semantics. Complete canonical receivers determine the functionals. Each endpoint uses its own physical support; field deltas use aligned original-member/local receiver identities and common valid support. Saved observation labels remain separate from any harmonized re-extraction of saved fields.

Four FIT families and all four of their actions are visited each epoch. A shuffled family cycle covers all FIT48 families over twelve epochs. The case provider distributes these families across the native optimizer updates and scales their loss contributions so that the epoch mean is the equal family/action mean, independent of native batch count. Each action is backpropagated before the next action is prepared. The same maintained optimizer performs native and auxiliary learning; no response-only head or optimizer is added. Histories record response exposure separately from primary case visits, and paired fitting is not sparse-only execution.
