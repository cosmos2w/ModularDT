## Explicit auxiliary action roles

`ThermalActionCorpus` accepts an external `thermal_response_actions_v1` manifest with baseline `cases` and explicit `actions`. Each case declares `case_id`, `source_case_dir` and its original `module_count` or `member_ids`. Each action declares `case_id`, a unique `action`, `role` (`fit` or `diagnostic`), `kind`, signed direction/amplitude `cell`, `sign`, `raw_case_dir` and `record_directory`; fitting actions additionally supply the maintained physical `J`/`P` finite changes in `physical_delta`. The existing `response_pair` loader reads these records using original-member identities, unchanged complete native observations and endpoint/common masks. The original four-action FIT48 and response-holdout corpus retains its own membership and selector.

Optional `thermal_state_response.pilot` selects `manifest_path`, `arm` (`control` or `coverage`) and an explicit positive `coefficient`. `control` repeats both signs of first-member `v0/a0` edits. `coverage` cycles both signs through `v0/a0`, `v1/a0` and `v0/a1`, with stable layout offsets; single-member layouts receive identical `v0/a0` edits in both arms. Crossed cells, second-member edits and joint edits cannot enter pilot fitting. Selection is a pure function of the epoch and ordered input IDs and consumes no training RNG. Histories expose the actually processed case/action IDs separately from original FIT-family exposure.

Pilot losses occupy the separate `response_pilot/*` namespace and preserve the existing endpoint, field-response and complete J/P weights and paired full/P95 reductions. Original FIT supervision remains its equal 16-action mean; the pilot contributes `coefficient` times its separate equal-action mean. For twelve pilot actions, coefficient `12/16` gives every pilot action the same per-action weight as each original FIT action, without diluting the original objective. Explicit `thermal_state_response.response_scales` restores saved original-FIT denominators for a fresh matched comparison, and supplied checkpoint scales must agree exactly. Pilot supervision rejects missing saved denominators instead of estimating scales from its new labels. TRAIN normalization, model initialization, physical sampling and an authorized execution budget remain separate required comparison controls; enabling this option grants no training permission.
