# Thermal model development protocol

Use the input-stratified membership and original case order declared by the selected recipe. Existing TRAIN150 and exposed validation manifests are external scientific artifacts; preserve their exact identities rather than creating replacements from model errors. Full-population training uses the declared TRAIN600 membership. TRAIN-only normalization, source split separation and exact resume identity are mandatory.

An epoch visits the declared training membership once. Distinguish short execution qualification from a substantive TRAIN150 learning comparison. The former uses only the epochs needed for execution/gradient/recovery checks and retains an aggregate 500-epoch technical ceiling; the latter normally targets 1,000 per explicitly planned configuration, with reviews by 100, at 500 and at 1,000. TRAIN150 can support development choices but does not establish mature full-data architecture quality. The evidence and staged budget rules are in [Thermal training budget conventions](Thermal_Training_Budget_Conventions.md).

A selected fresh TRAIN600 model normally starts with 100 epochs for preliminary evidence and operational handoff. For a separately authorized longer comparison, plan intermediate 500 and substantial 1,000-epoch reviews; a 2,000-epoch review needs a specific learning/schedule question. These horizons do not grant continuation authority or prove convergence. Follow [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) and the user's latest stop; R17-3 formal runs stop at 100 for the user's decision.

Compare original-member geometry and IDs, initialization, query sampling, objectives, schedules and case/epoch budgets. Keep aggregate accuracy, fieldwise/local errors, organizer use and complete runtime/memory separate. Do not interpret sparsity, support, a small directory or a short preliminary trajectory as physical causality, convergence or end-to-end savings.

Save complete useful histories, initial/current loss curves in `plots/training/`, latest state, one best-field selector and declared 100-epoch milestones. Keep figures out of `logs/`; record stable identity once and omit routine per-epoch sampling/order hashes or repeated identity dumps unless specifically needed. Refresh curves every ten epochs and at clean stops. Separate sampled training reductions, full exposed validation metrics and gradient diagnostics. The [run guide](Run_Artifacts_and_Monitoring.md) describes layouts and compatibility.

On the maintained workstation, the top level of `Trained_Results/ThermalChannel/HONF_Forward_Runs/` is reserved for formal TRAIN600 runs, with aliases `<run_name>` bound to DATA2 payloads. Preserve useful TRAIN150, qualification/debug and failed lineages under `_bk/<round>/<category>/<run_name>` or the round's ignored diagnostics directory. Remove useless disposable outputs only under the repository cleanup rules; preserve scientific checkpoints, resume state and failure provenance. Classify historical runs from saved membership and scientific role, and record alias relocations without rewriting their payloads or identities. Other installations must configure their own external data and output locations.

Keep each development round's reports together under `HONF_Proj/docs/reports/<round>/`, accessible through the repository's `docs/reports/` alias. Use milestone/topic subdirectories within that common directory. `HONF_Proj/UpgradePlan/` holds development plans rather than reports. Keep numerical evidence, runs, intermediate data, caches and one-time helpers in separate ignored DATA2 output locations; link to them from the report and retain curated figure PNG previews and PDF masters with the report. These local plans, reports and absolute machine recipes are not shipped as current launch instructions.
