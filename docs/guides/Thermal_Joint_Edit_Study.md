# Thermal frozen-predictor joint edits

The joint-edit study tests whether native overlapping organization improves coordinated two-module proposals under a fixed predictor. It is a finite-pool design-distribution prototype, not a fitted inverse policy, arbitrary-target generator, physical-causality claim, or evidence of independent generalization. The exposed DEV families and their historical use must be described explicitly. The reusable implementation is in `channelthermal.inverse.joint_edit_sampling`, `joint_edit_predictor`, and `joint_edit_reference`; the study and physical-reference entry points are `tools/thermal_joint_edit_study.py` and `tools/thermal_joint_edit_physical.py`. These tools do not themselves authorize a new study, GPU use, or physical solves.

## Predictor and identity

Use the literal selected P64 epoch-100 checkpoint from its source-age-5000 lineage and the local asset declared in that checkpoint. Load the inherited TRAIN normalization; do not fit normalization or model weights during this study. Each distinct design is prepared and scored afresh with the restricted P95 routed read, native material smooth-peak temperature and native pressure-drop functional. Frozen-local T/q is a diagnostic derived from the same 64 outside values, not the primary request score. A full-read replay is a separate diagnostic.

Preserve all original members, heat, materials, controls, E coordinates and physical measures. The case adapter maps original string module IDs to integer tensor slot keys; retain both in matching order. The E lattice has 24 by 8 addresses and cell lengths `(Lx/24, Ly/8)`, distinct from the physical receiver grid. Extract organization from the same ordinary preparation used by the predictor. Full access Π, retained access D, and actual directed applied contextual relations G are different quantities: keep packed row membership, weights, measures and structural self, and keep the MM, ME, EM and EE incidence and receiver/donor IDs with their actual stage identities. A transposed lookup does not imply a reverse exchange.

## Proposal law

Choose an unordered pair of present editable modules uniformly for each draw. Reuse the pair, independent coordinate noise ε, E-address-indexed noise z and nomination uniforms across the independent I, geometry G and organization H arms. Independent blocks use distinct seeds. Unselected members remain unchanged. With `u_iad = (x_id - x_ad)/cell_d`, the geometry loading is `exp(-||u_ia||²/2) tanh(u_iad)`. The organization loading is `D_ai tanh(u_iad + 0.25 Σ_j G_MM(i←j)(x_jd-x_id)/cell_d)`, using all present original M donors. The independent loading is zero.

Normalize each member-coordinate row over E addresses as `b = H/max(sqrt(Σ_a H²), 1e-8)` and write `v = Σ_a b²`. The selected displacement is `0.05 r [sqrt(1-ρ²v) ε + ρ Σ_a b_a z_a]`, with `ρ=1/sqrt(2)`. Before rejection, each selected coordinate has variance `(0.05 r)²`; the covariance reflects shared address noise, including possible opposite signs. Tiny or zero rows retain their independent residual. Reject a draw when either module moves more than `0.15 r` or the existing exact domain and minimum-gap checks fail. Never clip, repair, or refill; record attempted, valid, distinct and scored counts separately.

## Requests, phases and physical budget

Both requests require a compatible known unchanged-design physical baseline `(J0,P0)` and use baseline-anchored model changes. Let `sT=max(J0-Tin,0.01 TRAIN_temperature_scale)` and `sP=max(|P0|,0.01 FIT_baseline_pressure_RMS)`. Request A asks for `J≤J0-0.01sT` with `P≤P0+0.05sP`; request B asks for `P≤P0-0.01sP` with `J≤J0`. The energy is the sum of squared positive normalized objective shortfall and constraint violation; stable `exp(-energy)` weights resample the entire geometry-valid distinct pool, including predicted misses.

Each standard cell offers two blocks of 32 draws. FIT combines both blocks and selects one weighted nomination. After the FIT review, DEV selects one weighted nomination from block 1 and one distinct-uniform nomination from block 2. Save every DEV nomination before starting any DEV physical solve. Empty pools are failed slots; repeated exact designs may reuse a physical result and must not be replaced. The `--first-cell-attempts` option reduces the first case/request cell equally across arms when prior attempts must be accounted for; the default is 64 and the recipe records the effective setting. Keep any earlier attempt evidence and count it against the declared study budget.

The R15-7 physical ceiling is 96 started attempts, including failures, retries and aborted starts: up to 24 FIT nominations, 48 DEV nominations, 8 missing unchanged baselines and 16 retries of the same failed nomination or recipe. The unchanged baseline is a reference and fallback, not an improved generated design. Reuse a saved physical baseline only when its design, controls, source membership, solver recipe, native receiver identities, coordinates, masks and extraction agree; otherwise use an authorized baseline attempt. The analytic-wake reference is the dataset reference model, not independent CFD. Report convergence, physical request attainment, field fidelity, response errors and preparation, decode, local and reference costs separately; a surrogate-selected proposal or learned association alone does not establish physical improvement.
