# HET-MF physical and data contract

The native sheet is the unit square in dimensionless fixed reference units. The saved environment consists of 12 × 12 control records for electrical sheet conductance `s`, thermal sheet conductance `kappa`, and cooling `beta`; the solver reconstructs positive graded fields by log-bilinear interpolation with border clamping. Contact records contain center, radius, total conductance `gamma`, and terminal voltage `u`. Contacts have normalized finite-area quartic footprints and insulating lateral boundaries.

The reference first solves the finite-volume electrical system, then deposits its unique-face Joule energy and finite-contact loss into the sheet and solves the thermal system. Raw arrays include potential, temperature rise, separate sheet/contact heat, and oriented face current/heat flux. Processed fields use `[potential, jx_cell, jy_cell, temperature_rise, q_total]` in native `[y,x]` order. Cell current reconstructions are visualization or model targets, not substitutes for face-flux balance. Source labels, solver diagnostics, and hashes are excluded from model inputs.

The pilot is development-exposed and is not an independent formal TEST set. Standard and Expanded formal identities and completion receipts are recorded in `dataset_manifest.json`; their standalone training archives use `hetmf_training_h5_v1`. No HONF model has been fitted, no learned graph or inverse design has been validated, and physical-unit calibration remains outside this case.

The standalone archives contain canonical physical input records, unnormalized float32 targets, native queries and physical measures, explicit core/panel/audit memberships, immutable source lineage, and FP64 normalization fitted only on ordinary TRAIN. Model inputs are the prescribed contacts, material controls, masks, and query locations. Complete archive verification seals numerical data and provenance; the loader opens HDF5 handles lazily per process and binds query draws to physical case identity. Dataset and normalization fingerprints must accompany later checkpoints.

The case-local HONF adapter maps contact features `[r,log(gamma),u]` and material features `[log(s),log(kappa),log(beta)]` to the general two-dimensional `SceneBatch`/`QueryBatch` contracts. All five targets use the general nonlinear head; affine thermal response is disabled because voltage produces quadratic heat and temperature responses. The tested full-access content-attention model is a connection diagnostic with zero optimizer steps. See the [generation guide](../docs/HETMF_GENERATION_GUIDE.md) for dataset construction, verification and interpretation limits.
