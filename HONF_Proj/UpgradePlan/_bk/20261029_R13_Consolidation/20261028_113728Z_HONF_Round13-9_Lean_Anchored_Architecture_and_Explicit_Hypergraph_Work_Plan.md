## Model equations and the meaning of a hyperedge

For E receiver a, retain the actual applied final-cycle incoming relations, with their separate stage provenance:

\[
G_a=[G^{E\leftarrow M}_a,\;G^{E\leftarrow E}_a],\qquad S_{ai}=\mathbf1_{i=E_a},\qquad w_{ai}=(G_{ai}+S_{ai})\mu_i,\qquad R_{ai}=\frac{w_{ai}}{\sum_j w_{aj}}.
\]

The current member measure gives equal discrete measure to each present M with unit total within M, and normalized positive physical cell measures with unit total within E. It is an access measure, distinct from native physical integration measures. Structural self S is a declared access convention, not learned EE self. MM/EM/EE/ME stage records describe actual ordered exchanges; the captured EM and EE blocks do not represent one simultaneous physical influence matrix.

For coverage τ, use one whole-row joint M/E prefix and retain the owning E:

\[
C_a(\tau)=\operatorname{Prefix}_{\tau}(R_a)\cup\{E_a\},\qquad C_{a,t}=C_a\cap t,\quad t\in\{M,E\}.
\]

Take the shortest positive-weight prefix in descending R order, with the existing role-then-original-ID tie behavior, then append ownE if absent. Keep positive self access and legal empty M reads. At fixed R the supports for90/95/99 must be nested, including mandatory self. The final retained mass can exceed τ, particularly after ownE is appended. Never retain τ independently within each role, impose an M winner, or apply another hidden entrance truncation. The current compiler already accepts `retained_mass`; audit serialization, execution, paired training and evaluator propagation before adding another setting.

The inherited typed read is

\[
p_{ai\mid t}(q)=\frac{\mathbf1_{i\in C_{a,t}}R_{ai}e^{\ell_{ai}(q)}}{\sum_{j\in C_{a,t}}R_{aj}e^{\ell_{aj}(q)}},\qquad c_t(q)=\sum_a\lambda_a(q)\rho_{a,t}(q)\sum_i p_{ai\mid t}(q)V_i,\qquad \hat u(q)=F(q,c_M(q),c_E(q)).
\]

A has one nonzero λ; L retains its smooth local cover. Keep L's per-row calibration, normalization and strength before blending complete contexts. Combining its priors first would change the equations. Original identities, positions, physical measures and context features survive until q-dependent reading. A final weighted reduction after q selects and scores members is a legitimate set operation, not the earlier pooled-only bottleneck.

State the current division of responsibilities plainly: whole-row R determines joint admission; within-role p determines member composition; continuous ρ determines typed context amplitude. A common positive row scale cancels inside p, while the M/E share in R does not automatically become the M/E amplitude share after typed normalization. This is an explicit design choice to inspect, not an inconsistency to repair by silently multiplying context by another mass factor. Earlier A/B changes altered this meaning and degraded fidelity. A normalization removal must preserve these semantics or be evaluated as a separate equation change, with the native local-temperature evidence retained.

Define an explicit receiver hyperedge by original-member identities, incidence weights, structural-self annotation, stage provenance, service region and permitted read operation. Distinguish its learned positive-G membership, compiled C membership and actual query-used positive-β membership instead of treating them as one support. Its members can overlap other hyperedges. A row with only one accessible member is a legitimate degenerate case, not a many-member success. Report edge cardinality and query use rather than relabelling rows as discovered physical groups. The architecture supports multi-member integration through a common context and joint nonlinear predictor; demonstrate the utility of the member combination with controlled measurements. Neither calling attention a hypergraph nor nonlinearity in the output head alone establishes useful higher-order organization.
