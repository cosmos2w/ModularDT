## Applied organization, explicit E self-access, and directory compilation

Rows use receiver-first semantics. For E anchor a, the learned row is the actual final-cycle incoming relationship

\[
G_a=[\,H^{\mathrm{applied}}_{E\leftarrow M,a:},\ H^{\mathrm{applied}}_{E\leftarrow E,a:}\,].
\]

Use the captured EM and EE blocks, not a transposed ME index or an inferred multicycle influence graph. MM and ME continue doing their actual encoder work; final contextual M values remain available when a directory admits them. Preserve stage/cycle provenance, masks, original IDs and the literal applied EE diagonal.

Add one separately recorded structural self-access coefficient for the anchor, matching the existing M identity coefficient convention:

\[
S^E_{ai}=\mathbf1_{i=\mathrm{ownE}(a)},\qquad
B^{\mathrm{access}}_{ai}=(G_{ai}+S^E_{ai})\mu_i,\qquad
R^{\mathrm{access}}_{ai}=\frac{B^{\mathrm{access}}_{ai}}{\sum_j B^{\mathrm{access}}_{aj}}.
\]

Use the existing role measure μ exactly once: present M records have equal discrete measure totaling one; eligible E records have positive physical measure normalized within E. Normalize the whole M+E row, not the blocks separately. Keep applied G, structural S and derived access weights separate in traces. S is a permission to access the original anchor record, not an extra encoder exchange. If learned EE self is positive, it remains in G and the explicit structural term is added once; never duplicate that actual learned message in another ledger.

For a valid positive-measure E, this guarantees positive self access, not a normalized minimum percentage or dominance. Do not add a tunable self quota, fixed residual budget, strength floor or new geometric self-bias in the first comparison. Record actual self prior and post-score mass, including its resolution dependence. An otherwise empty learned row becomes a structural-self-only directory and is labelled that way, not presented as learned multi-member organization.

Compile one stable joint prefix per E:

\[
C_a=\operatorname{Prefix}_{95\%}(R^{\mathrm{access}}_a)\cup\{\mathrm{ownE}(a)\}.
\]

This retains at least 95% of the whole row's M+E access mass and may add at most one mandatory anchor beyond the prefix. Use existing role/original-ID tie ordering. M may be entirely absent; E is intentionally present as the local anchor. Do not force separate 95% retention for M/E or silently retain 95% of the nonself remainder. Store full legal support F_a, C_a, selected original IDs and live selected weights; row normalization and sorting happen once per prepared scene/E, not once per query. Compile only needed E rows lazily if that is simpler and actually cheaper, while preserving the same directory.

The directory is scene-conditioned. Rebuild after changed physical inputs, controls or parameters. Reuse it among queries in that scene preparation; do not detach learned coefficients, freeze organization between fits or persist stale member features. Discrete shortlist indices need no surrogate gradient, while selected weights and member states remain live. Paired full/restricted training supplies the same native tasks through F_a and C_a and helps retain learning signal to omitted positive relationships.

## A/L member integration and the boundary-safe fusion rule

For a candidate anchor a and role t, compute the existing raw query/member score x_i(q) only for admitted original records. Calibrate it using that anchor's selected row prior and candidate set, retaining the current bounded prior-weighted moments. With ell_at,i(q) so defined,

\[
p_{at,i}(q)=\frac{\mathbf1_{i\in C_a\cap t}R^{\mathrm{access}}_{ai}e^{\ell_{at,i}(q)}}
{\sum_{j\in C_a\cap t}R^{\mathrm{access}}_{aj}e^{\ell_{at,j}(q)}},\qquad
c_{at}(q)=\rho_{at}(q)\sum_i p_{at,i}(q)V_i.
\]

The same final p_at supplies expectation/entropy to the existing U typed-strength network and supplies the value read; retain the full-scene eligible-count entropy denominator, bounded continuous strength, exact empty role and invalid-query behavior. No parallel O distribution or A-style joint role-amplitude budget is introduced. For full reading, substitute F_a everywhere; full and restricted calibration stays separate.

A reads one anchor. L blends **completed per-anchor contexts** before one joint trunk/head:

\[
c_t(q)=\sum_{a\in\mathcal A(q)}\lambda_a(q)c_{at}(q),\qquad
\widehat y(q)=F\bigl(q,c_M(q),c_E(q)\bigr).
\]

Do not renormalize λ over only anchors with a nonempty role: an M contribution present in just one anchor must fade with that anchor's geometric weight. Do not blend full priors and reapply joint95, normalize role mass after geometric blending, blend final predictions instead of contexts, or call historical H on the union candidate mask. Those are different equations and can reintroduce support jumps or permit a row to read members it excluded from its own C_a.

Deduplicate the union of active anchors' candidates by (role, original ID), score each raw q/member pair once, and share original values. Keep row-local candidate masks, calibration, probabilities and strengths when scattering results back to anchor reads. Coalesce final coefficients b_ti=Σ_a λ_a rho_at p_at,i onto original members before value reduction where equivalent. L has small deterministic geometric fusion, not another learned entrance network. There is no pre-query pooled value replacing the members.

A/L must execute row gathers and packed/segmented member operations. Do not manufacture one-hot entrance tensors and retain global entrance MLPs, dense πR, all-address support scans or H's three full-address contractions. A dense implementation of exactly the same A/L equations is permitted only as a small numerical oracle. Share scene projections, stable ID order and query affine work from R13-7 where dependencies agree; avoid repeated CPU counters inside tiles/replay.

In paired training, shared raw scores may cover the full support already paid for by the full branch, with work charged once. Standalone restricted inference must score only its actual candidate union. Reusing full scores to make restricted inference appear sparse is not allowed. Report unique raw score rows separately from per-anchor normalizations, memberships and reductions.
