## Source-level diagnosis: three separate operations

The relevant source is [applied incidence](../src/honf_forward_core/interaction_reused_member_routing/core.py), [head calibration](../src/honf_forward_core/joint_native/routing.py), [positive gate](../src/honf_forward_core/continuous_joint/primitives.py), [actual contextual reduction](../src/honf_forward_core/continuous_joint/preparation.py), [ordered exchange and capture](../src/honf_forward_core/content_hypergraph/staged_attention_v2.py), [member measure](../src/honf_forward_core/interaction_reused_member_routing/joint.py) and [anchored directory/read](../src/honf_forward_core/interaction_reused_member_routing/anchored.py).

For an eligible receiver a, donor i and head h, let s be the actual normalized-QK score including geometry, before donor log measure. The current route calibration and incidence are

\[
d_{hai}=s_{hai}-\max_{j\in\mathcal E_a}s_{haj},\qquad x_{hai}=\gamma d_{hai}+b,\qquad G_{ai}=\psi\!\left(\sum_h\omega_h x_{hai}\right),\quad \psi(x)=\frac{[x]_+^2}{1+[x]_+^2}.
\]

Here ω is the learned convex head mixture, γ the existing bounded positive scale and b the learned route bias. This is an exact-zero positive-quadratic gate, **not the old query entmax/null reader**. An EE member supported by one head can disappear after averaging negative relative scores from other heads. Negative gate inputs have zero local gate derivative. Shared parameters, row maxima, contextual softmax and other active paths can still change scores and reopen an edge; do not describe zero support as mathematically permanent or all upstream gradients as absent.

A read-only CPU check of the completed `round13_9_20261028/train150/thermal_r13_9a_e_anchor_inherited_train150_v1/checkpoints/epoch_1000_model.pt` gives EE scale0.99865, bias1.45252 and four head weights0.24812/0.25069/0.25271/0.24848. This endpoint is not an all-closed negative-bias route or a single dominant-head mixture. Its gate keeps relationships whose weighted average score gap is sufficiently close to the separate head maxima. Quantify the corresponding stage-input disagreement before attributing the narrow EE support to one unique cause.

The actual contextual message uses a different coefficient:

\[
A_{hai}=\operatorname{softmax}_{i\in\mathcal E_a}(s_{hai}+\log m_i),\qquad z_{ha}=\sum_i A_{hai}G_{ai},\qquad c_{hai}=\frac{A_{hai}G_{ai}}{z_{ha}+10^{-3}(1-z_{ha})^2}.
\]

The captured reusable G is the applied incidence gate used in this reduction. It is not the normalized per-head coefficient c or a measured physical influence. Native donor measure enters A, while the access directory currently applies its own dimensionless measure to G. Therefore the current access law is a questionable convention to compare, not an established duplicate-measure coding error.

For present roles, current access weights are

\[
\mu_{M_i}=1/N_M,\qquad \mu_{E_i}=a_i/\sum_{j\in E}a_j,\qquad w_{ai}=(G_{ai}+S_{ai})\mu_i,\qquad R_{ai}=w_{ai}/\sum_jw_{aj},\quad S_{ai}=\mathbf1_{i=E_a}.
\]

When few EE gates are positive, E's unit-total inventory measure can leave very little mass on those positive edges. Equal total measure before gating does not imply comparable mass after gating. Finally, the directory retains the shortest joint M/E prefix reaching τ and appends ownE if absent. This last operation can discard positive EE links, but **no choice of τ can recover G=0**. Separate upstream gate support, downstream access mass and truncation in the experiments.

## Candidate strategies: change one mechanism at a time

### P: comparable per-member access measure

First test a transparent alternative to inventory-unit-total measure, leaving all contextual exchange and applied G unchanged:

\[
\nu_{M_i}=1,\qquad \nu_{E_i}=\frac{a_i}{\bar a_E}=\frac{N_Ea_i}{\sum_{j\in E}a_j},\qquad w^{P}_{ai}=(G_{ai}+S_{ai})\nu_i,\qquad R^P_{ai}=w^P_{ai}/\sum_jw^P_{aj}.
\]

Use active counts and positive measures, not padded tensor widths. Empty M remains legal; structural ownE remains explicit and positive. For equal-area E, ν=1 for all original members, so this is simply access proportional to applied incidence plus declared self. Relative physical E-cell measures remain meaningful on unequal cells. This removes the old N_E versus N_M per-record attenuation without prescribing either role's total mass, a minimum E count or an M winner. It does not restore zero gates.

An illustrative calculation, not trained evidence: four M with G=0.5 each, eight nonself E with G=0.5 each,192 equal E cells and structural self1 yield current M/nonself-E/self mass shares95.05%/3.96%/0.99%. P yields28.57%/57.14%/14.29%. This is a substantial equation change, not a free correction. Measure effects on M selection, ownE dominance, native fields and complete runtime.

P also changes resolution semantics: splitting a cell into identical subcells can increase aggregate E access mass because per-record measures remain near one. Run a small measure/refinement fixture with conserved physical area and explicitly matched features/gates to expose that effect. Compare the same physical point/region with its split-cell children using aggregated joint R, admitted physical area at90/95/99, β and native predictions where the adapter permits. A same-row algebra fixture does not establish complete-model resolution invariance, since coordinates, gates and anchors can change too. Count/refinement behavior is part of strategy selection: retain P only with a defensible record-versus-area interpretation and acceptable measured changes over the tested discretizations. Do not market it as a universal physical quadrature rule or silently adapt the core to E=192.

### U: preserve specialist-head EE relationships before sparsification

Test a second, independent hypothesis by moving the convex head mixture **after** the existing sparse nonlinearity for EE:

\[
G^{U}_{ai}=\sum_h\omega_h\psi(x_{hai})\quad\text{for EE only}.
\]

Keep the existing head scores, masks, calibration parameters and parameter inventory. Run this gate in the actual EE contextual message computation in every existing cycle, capture the gate actually used, and reuse that same gate in the directory. MM/EM/ME, the read equation and the initial access measure remain unchanged in the isolated U arm. A post-hoc recalculation from an old snapshot is only an exploratory counterfactual, not evidence of implemented relation reuse.

On identical stage inputs, the old positive support is contained in U's positive support; individual head specialists can survive. For example, two equally weighted calibrated head inputs1.5 and−2.5 give old G=0 and U≈0.34615. All heads nonpositive still give zero, so U remains truly sparse without a forced degree or positive floor. The nonzero weights have no universal ordering because ψ is not globally convex. Full re-execution changes later states and scores, so neither support containment nor improved accuracy is guaranteed for complete scenes or training. Record the difference between identical-input stage analysis and actual re-prepared results.

This is the preferred upstream experiment because it directly tests head cancellation while retaining sparse execution. Quantify head disagreement, specialist edges rescued, their actual message mass/gradients and their eventual query use, rather than just counting extra positive gates. Preserve ordered EM/EE provenance; these stages use different contextual source states.

### X: combine only complementary validated changes

If P improves access comparability and U preserves useful EE relationships, test P+U as a separately named arm against the isolated arms. Do not assume benefits add. An access-only change can alter subsequent learned interaction, so retain both stage-fixed and trained comparisons. The final retained method can be U alone, P alone, X, or the original method if none improves the relevant tradeoff; preserve the best reusable implementation and diagnosis rather than forcing a larger hyperedge to win.

### Bounded alternatives, not an automatic larger search

If identical-stage analysis instead shows persistent all-head EE closure, one smooth EE gate using the same calibrated score, such as sigmoid, may replace the U experiment slot. It must affect actual contextual exchange and subsequent reuse, have no mandatory mass floor, and be measured for dense-support cost. Do not fit both several gate families and several temperatures. Prefer the existing `dense_weighted` contextual executor when a measured dense-gate case benefits and native output/gradient parity holds; this computes the same coefficient-weighted member reduction without a materialized edge-value bank, not an all-open substitution. Report dense work honestly.

If P's count/refinement dependence is unacceptable, investigate the alternative access interpretation **from actual applied contextual coefficients**, using a declared fixed head mean of the live c values from their actual EM/EE stages, concatenated across roles with structural self recorded separately. Current snapshots retain G and aggregate message mass, not the full per-head pair coefficients. Reconstructing c from stage-local scores, native measure, G and the exact guard is a diagnostic replay; an implemented coefficient-reuse path must retain/export the live coefficients actually used without detaching. Do not multiply these already measure-bearing coefficients by μ again. Route normalization makes EM/EE block masses often near one and therefore changes cross-role competition; adding S=1 is an explicit self scale, not a neutral identity. A head mean is a scalar access convention, not the exact signed projected vector contribution. Capturing live coefficients adds preparation/gradient/storage work. Test only if these semantics and costs answer a demonstrated problem, and let it replace P's fitting slot rather than creating a fifth thermal arm. For equal-area cells, an extra raw-G access arm duplicates P and should not be run.

No per-role95% lists, equal-M/E-mass renormalization, fixed E quota, geometric-neighbor floor, all-E epsilon padding, frozen gate period, target K, new auxiliary sparsity loss or compulsory pooled region representation is a default candidate. Preserve useful remote cooperation. A geometric distance map can motivate a diagnosis but cannot label an edge useless on its own.
