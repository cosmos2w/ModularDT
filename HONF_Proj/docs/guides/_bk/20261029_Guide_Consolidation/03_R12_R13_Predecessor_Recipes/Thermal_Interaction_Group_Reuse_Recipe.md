## Matched arms and decisions

The versioned configurations are [G-EE](../../../../../configs/research/thermal_interaction_groups_gee_dev_v1.json), [G-F](../../../../../configs/research/thermal_interaction_groups_gf_dev_v1.json) and [G-H](../../../../../configs/research/thermal_interaction_groups_gh_dev_v1.json). G-EE alone adds E→E after M→E. G-F and G-EE use known singleton partitions and execute neither candidate construction nor the stop policy. G-H nominates a spatial merge tree from the applied M→E relation and contextual E states and samples a reversible frontier from epoch 1. The common named model tensors have identical seeded initialization; extra E→E tensors are initialized afterward.

Use the existing fixed25 TRAIN150/exposed DEV22 binding and selected-TRAIN normalization. Every arm uses seed 0, FP32, width 128, four heads, two layers, Q1024, effective batch 48, nominal microbatch 24 and AdamW 3e-4 with weight decay 1e-5. An epoch visits all 150 selected TRAIN cases. The initial endpoint is 100, with 15,000 primary case visits, 400 optimizer boundaries and 15.36 million primary fluid queries. Native surface/material queries and finite-response endpoints are additional work. The absolute schedule is 1,000 epochs; it does not authorize continuation. Native loss weights remain the declared fixed weights at and after epoch 101, rather than silently inheriting another runner's amendment.

Existing physical-reference family 0348 is the only response fitting anchor. A no-step response gradient calibration at the first macro fixes the coefficient targeting 10% of the shared encoder/head native gradient, with the existing downward cap applied at every response event. Every fourth primary update adds one alternating heat/single-geometry response pair before the same Adam step. There are 100 response microbatches and 200 endpoint-scene visits in a completed 100-epoch arm, plus the separately counted calibration pair. Other atlas families remain reference-only or response-validation-only. No solver calls, M1/M2 response references, mixed-geometry training or flow-invariance constraints are introduced.

Train G-EE and G-F first. Compare their explicit epoch-100 checkpoints on all nine native DEV22 boards, the fixed six-M1/six-M2 TRAIN panel and existing physical responses. Priority temperature/peak preservation bands are mean ratio ≤1.10 and p90 ratio ≤1.15; response bands use the ratio of pooled-record medians and p90 absolute RMSE for each priority component. Always retain absolute errors, strata, tails and near-null exclusions. Compare with the fixed selected-TRAIN mean native predictor and zero-response predictor. A preservation ratio alone cannot qualify an inaccurate surrogate. A material field/response failure diagnoses the communication experiment. It does not prevent a separately reasoned retained-E→E adaptive comparison under the authorized recovery budget.

## Adaptive frontier and counted work

G-H has no forced-leaf warmup, organizer freeze, target K, risk head, stopped tie loss or expected-K reward. Temperature decreases continuously from 2 to 1 over absolute epochs 1–500; the work multiplier follows the same cosine from 0 to 1. Its value near epoch 100 is only 0.094. The connected 24×8 grid has 352 candidate edges per layer. Tree storage consists of compact children and additive statistics, followed by one ancestry-label traversal. Expensive group transformations and key/value projections apply only to active frontier nodes.

Before any adaptive training, fit positive relation/state/coordinate distance scales on at most 12 input-selected TRAIN scenes, measure stage prices, and qualify the task/work policy VJPs on the same complete encoder/organizer parameter inventory. Record the calibration receipt and fixed prices in an ordinary executable recipe. A null or unstable work gradient leaves its coefficient zero and work learning unqualified; a zero-price arm may test native-driven adaptation but must be labelled as having unqualified work pressure. The work policy contribution targets 2% of the nonzero native-policy norm and is capped downward at 10% of the full native gradient norm. Conditional native gradients and score-function terms are separate; previous-update TRAIN baselines are action-independent and checkpointed. Epoch 100 reviews do not silently retune prices.

Query work scores at most all M lightweight proposal keys, selects two M addresses, visits at most eight postings and nine spatial cells, retains at most four geometric group entrances and at most eight total group candidates, then scores and reads only these retained group values. A deterministic cyclic alternative occupies one proposal slot on one in sixteen TRAIN query IDs when possible. Evaluation uses deterministic learned proposals. The ordinary reader has zero original-E and zero M-value reads. Preparation still embeds and contextualizes original E and charges both layers' group formation, relation reduction and index construction. A small final K alone is neither a complete work metric nor evidence of physical causality.

`balanced_postings_v1` normalizes the nonempty four-cell geometric window to unit mass and each module's existing posting B to a conditional distribution; original nullable proposal coefficients still weight the module entrances. It changes no query cap, physical input, value bank or native loss. Existing B coverage is reported before conditioning; normalization cannot recover omitted relationships. The exhaustive diagnostic now includes each B contribution once. Compare ordinary restricted postings with restricted candidates under full B to isolate weighting, then compare that fixed full-B formula with exhaustive candidate coverage. In balanced mode both full-B diagnostics normalize by full per-module B mass, while ordinary production normalizes over postings. Full-B diagnostics score prepared groups only; production remains bounded. Old exhaustive metrics are retained as superseded buggy evidence. The execution evaluator also replays a reached node as parent versus legal child descent through every dependent later layer and native extraction; this measures physical sharing utility rather than assuming generic parent/child equivalence.

Adaptive native and response endpoint frontiers are sampled from the first update, with separate previous-update native/work/response baselines. Finite-response score gradients include both endpoint log probabilities; conditional derivatives and the existing response gradient cap remain. Deterministic evaluation is reported separately. The no-step TRAIN12 calibration records distance component scales, nonnegative measured stage prices, full parameter-slice gradients and uncertainty in the local evidence receipt; the ordinary adaptive recipe stores the resulting fixed values. The work proxy prices executed preparation and query counts, including an irreducible E-row term. It is a calibration approximation, not measured runtime or a guarantee of useful compression.
