# Joint native HONF: future physical data specification

**Archived reference — 29 October 2026.** This guide records a predecessor model or a dated experiment, not current architecture policy or standing launch authorization. Use the [current guide index](../../../README.md); see the [archive review](../README.md) for the reason it was moved.

This is an inert specification for a separately authorized study. Round9 does not generate a dataset, run a reference solver, optimize a layout, change its native environment catalogue, or train an inverse generator. A working E value path, learned typed support, and an original-input sensitivity are operational model evidence; independently prescribed environmental reference states are needed to identify physical M–E responses.

## Existing evidence and its scope

The bounded Round9 inventory verifies the four original TRAIN Thermal response families 0001/M3, 0318/M5, 0333/M7 and 0348/M10 against the exact NPZ hashes inherited from the D8 provider. Every family retains a baseline, eight geometry endpoints forming a complete 3-by-3 stencil for two module displacements, and positive/negative heat-transfer endpoints. These records use the existing analytic-wake flow approximation and shared-grid thermal generator, with native 128-by-64 fluid queries, module-joined interface angles and normalized material-disk coordinates. They retain dataset units and a heat-flux proxy; they are not coupled Navier–Stokes CFD or an SI heat-flux reference. Baseline-to-positive heat differences are exposed supervised TRAIN evidence.

Each family's signed heat-transfer endpoints span one requested heating direction, not two independent excitations. Small float32 asymmetry in the stored opposite increments must not be counted as a second scientific direction. A separate saved 0001 panel contains baseline, independent +0.125 perturbations on modules 0 and 1, and their combined endpoint, with numerical mixed deltas and retained raw-state paths. It permits a heat–heat affine-null check at fixed geometry; it supplies no independently varied environment. The family geometry stencils contain compatible baseline, individual and joint displacement endpoints, so geometry–geometry finite interactions are available for a labelled exposed-TRAIN replay. A geometry-plus-heat combined reference endpoint was not found in the inspected family catalogues.

One historical generated-M3 layout has a frozen six-candidate pool with verified saved designs, converged reference metadata, scalar objectives and raw-state paths. Its outcomes were used in a previous study, so a future Round9 replay is exposed decision evidence. Reuse its original constraints, pressure sections, temperature objective and finite-pool denominator; do not reinterpret the pool as an independent test or a newly validated inverse generator. The bounded inventory does not claim that the other historical generated layouts have comparable frozen common pools.

The retained receipt `physical_response_inventory.json` under the ignored Round9 evidence root records exact paths, hashes, labels, native IDs, units, masks, exposure and missing endpoints. It establishes what can be replayed. Actual Round9 response agreement, candidate ranking and scientific qualification require their own measured results at matched literal checkpoints. No controlled independent environmental field perturbations were found in the inspected Thermal response/provider inputs or Wind provider contract.

## Required factorial configurations

Choose legitimate prescribed environmental variables before observing model errors. Examples for a future Thermal generator include an inlet profile, inlet/background temperature or boundary transport property that the generator truly accepts and the public adapter represents without reading targets. The present geometry-derived E features are not independent actuators. For Wind, separately define the inflow profile or boundary condition and reference frame at fixed turbine geometry; stored direction rows alone do not create an identified environmental experiment.

| Question | Required saved reference configurations | Variables held fixed | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independent M–E response | Baseline, module intervention a, environmental intervention b, and combined a+b | Native module/receiver correspondence, unaffected boundaries, material law, solver/extraction settings | Four-state finite interaction, separated from each single response |
| Geometry–heat response | Baseline, geometry change, heat change and their combination | All other operating inputs and exact source heat ordering | Geometry-conditioned heat response; currently missing a combined endpoint in the inspected families |
| Fixed-geometry heat null | Baseline, independent source changes a and b, and a+b | Layout, environment and linear thermal coefficients | Numerical affine-null test, with stopping and float32 error reported |
| Environmental resolution transfer | The same physical prescribed field represented on declared regular and irregular input quadratures | Reference solution, physical measures and receiver coordinates | Discretization/representation evidence, separated from hard-route reselection |
| Design decision | A frozen finite feasible candidate set and reference endpoints for every candidate | Constraint definitions, physical objectives and evaluation protocol | Feasibility, ranks and reference regret within the actual finite pool |

For M–E tests, save at least two independently chosen module directions and two environmental directions with their crossed endpoints on each selected layout. Record the actual perturbation matrix and its singular values with an input-precision-aware rank tolerance. Opposite signs, repeated amplitude scaling and coordinate transformations do not add independent physical directions. Choose magnitudes from physical feasibility and reference resolution before model comparison, and include a second smaller magnitude to assess the finite-difference regime when the separately approved solve budget permits it.

## Data and physics contracts

Every record must carry a type-qualified source catalogue, immutable family/action IDs, native coordinates and characteristic lengths, original physical measures, prescribed values and their units, partition/exposure, input quantization, full baseline/endpoint configuration hashes, reference generator/version, mesh/time/closure settings, stopping diagnostics and raw final-state provenance. Keep raw prescribed E values distinct from geometry-derived features and contextualized model states. The deployed adapter must construct inputs without opening solved fields or objective labels.

Save each reference field and valid mask on a declared common Eulerian support, together with module-joined surface and material supports. A moved module changes the physical locations of its surface/material queries; preserve the original IDs and local-coordinate joins and report their distinction from fixed-fluid queries. Save role-specific units, full-field residuals and response signs/magnitudes. Recompute module maxima independently from each endpoint's material field and retain the original pressure-gauge/pressure-section convention. Do not interpolate a missing combined endpoint or relabel an unmatched geometry as a valid response pair.

For the current zero-background Thermal capability, flow and routing are heat-independent and temperature uses signed source coefficients with T(0)=0. Nonzero prescribed background temperatures require an explicit T0+Kh capability and data that identify T0. Temperature-dependent material properties, buoyancy, radiation or other nonlinear heating require their own physical capability and independently validated reference data; changing a neural head does not supply those references. Distinguish prescribed analytic-wake fields from any future coupled CFD source throughout a mixed-source study.

## Selection, evaluation and authorization

Freeze input-only family/layout membership, response directions, physical queries, solver settings, acceptance bands and objectives before fitting. Fit normalizers only on the selected TRAIN records. Keep exposed development families separate from locked independent TEST families, and avoid putting correlated directions or duplicate physical families in different partitions. All compared models must share the same reference endpoints, native measures and query draws.

Report field fidelity, operational E-to-M value influence, original-input dependence, four-state physical-response agreement, typed executed work, fair memory and complete latency separately. Evaluate rebuilt routing and validated fixed-route IDs on freshly rebuilt states; never bypass stale-state checks. For finite interactions use F(a+b)-F(a)-F(b)+F(0), with the four compatible references and their numerical resolution. A missing endpoint is unavailable evidence. Learned attention or native support does not itself establish a causal physical edge.

Before a future data campaign, obtain a separate decision specifying source fidelity, exact family/action allowlist, independent TRAIN/DEV/TEST handling, solver-attempt cap, elapsed/device allocation, disk peak/headroom, checkpoint/evidence retention and cleanup. This document supplies no launch command, executable generator or implicit additional solve allowance.
