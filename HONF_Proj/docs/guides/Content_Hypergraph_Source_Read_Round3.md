## Selected read and response interfaces

`source_read_policy="utility"` or `"completion"` opts into the shared selected decoder. The controller scores cached eight-wide source/query projections and the existing light geometry descriptor. The fine Fourier features and128-wide pair values follow the union of positive smooth utility/near gates. Flow retains original attention coefficients without renormalization. Thermal uses physically present source columns even when their attention measure is zero; it never attention-weights signed K. The completion uses bias-free rank16 query/source factors and adds selected fine-minus-base residuals; its scalar output bias contributes once over all present heating sources. The all-one gate endpoint calls the original full reader directly, preserving legacy arithmetic without cancellation through the completion.

The selected partial function projects headwise hidden reductions and composes its scalar Thermal projection. Arithmetic equivalence between its packed, dense-masked and degree-bucket packet executors must be checked separately from sparse-versus-full model distortion. A failed strict legacy-versus-reassociated FP32 qualification is retained and keeps the dense training baseline on legacy arithmetic; it does not become a looser sparse/full equivalence claim. Counters distinguish actual fine executions, selected rows, packet/dense padding, scoring, sorting and rank factors. The planner and all overhead remain inside complete-call timings. A fitted all-one fast path currently computes the light attention scores again inside the legacy reader; its two score passes are counted.

Use `core.source_read_override_v1(mode, executor=..., operating_point=...)` as a read-only context, preparing the organization inside the context. Modes are `full`, `fitted`, `attn95/99` and equal-count `nearest95/99`, `directional95/99`, `shuffle95/99`. These controls do not train another model. The directional diagnostic uses the declared downstream x axis; shuffle scores are deterministic functions of input physical positions, never learned source-ID embeddings. Entering or leaving a context invalidates old prepared organizations. Changing weights, router buffers, source/query IDs, transforms, query tensors or retained factors also requires rebuilding. Explicit query selection/reordering creates a new query request with remapped local support; it never silently reuses an old mask.

`nearest_fitted`, `directional_fitted` and `shuffle_fitted` instead match the fitted policy's positive physical-pair support count at each receiver, including protected near sources. They preserve original attention and the same rank completion for an S-Complete checkpoint. These read-only controls use unit gates on their selected pairs; fitted gates retain their smooth attenuation, so they are different approximation functions. Their measured work includes the fitted scorer used to discover the comparison count. The `95/99` controls match attention counts and must not be described as matching a learned policy's work unless those actual counts also agree.

The saved-response evaluator accepts `--source-read-policy full|fitted|attn95|attn99`, optional `--source-read-executor packed|packet|dense_masked` and `--source-read-operating-point 0|1`. Its default `checkpoint` follows the saved model. An override covers preparation, baseline and every endpoint together; it never fits thresholds or changes weights. The receipt records the actual response representation and dense scalar-kernel or selected/rank-factor entries. A full A2-D call can construct a dense scalar K internally even when no explicit dense export was requested; those two costs are distinct. Historical numerical outputs and saved receipts are unchanged.

Ordinary selected Thermal application, JVP and VJP use sparse signed coefficients and optional rank factors without constructing dense `[B,Q,M]` K. `export_source_kernels` is the separately priced explicit materialization path. Reusable selected affine queries require versioned tensors and preparation under `torch.no_grad()`; inference-mode tensors cannot certify later in-place changes. Endpoint heating must satisfy `abs(h)<=H`, where H is the fixed positive TRAIN-only envelope; an out-of-envelope endpoint requires explicit full mode. Tangents/increments may exceed that endpoint envelope. Native cached increment/JVP calls preserve this distinction. The native Thermal field standard deviation converts raw core temperature to physical temperature, so the teacher's raw K is already TRAIN-standardized temperature per heat and its auxiliary temperature scale is1.

Initial calibration uses at most256 model-target queries per TRAIN scene and256 new-parameter AdamW updates, with global-norm clipping at1. It records actual retention and context/K distortion for its two fixed operating points, including infeasible density targets caused by near protection. The primary point defaults to the less aggressive useful point. A fixed auxiliary coefficient is calibrated to at most5% of the initial native-gradient norm. One current-model TRAIN buffer of at most128 probes is refreshed at most every8 ordinary updates, with extra dense teacher rows strictly below5% of actual primary fine-row exposure. Full physical histories, inherited Adam age and the manager's separate clocks/cache are restored together on exact resume. The child CLI requires both `--source-read-child-declaration PATH` and `--branch-from-checkpoint PATH`; a sparse full1000 recipe remains fail-closed until its fresh warmup/calibration implementation is available and qualified.
