# HONF R13: consolidated development and evidence

R13 evolved from spatial group functions into a general joint-field predictor that exchanges information among original module (M) and environment (E) records, reuses actually applied relations to organize access, and reads individual records at query time. R13-5 established a strong matched-age accuracy and sparse-member-work result; R13-8 made E-anchored access substantially cheaper on measured workloads but exposed local-temperature losses; R13-11 reached the clearest structural result: S and X share a simple E-addressed, relation-reused, original-member joint predictor, with complementary field-fidelity and environmental-access emphases. They are the retained formal candidates and the foundation for subsequent development. R13-12 tested a module-mediated alternative and stopped it after broad fidelity and cost regressions. These results support continued evaluation of S and X; they do not establish that all predictor, organizer, efficiency or inverse goals have converged.

**Predictor.** R13-5 ME500 improved all nine canonical validation means and tails against independent R13-4 Guide500; R13-8 A/L500 preserved near aggregate accuracy but had materially worse peak and material temperature. R13-11 S1000 led the matched subset U/S/C comparison, while X999 gave a different access/fidelity tradeoff. The next decision needs literal full-population checkpoints, fieldwise and local errors, and consistent physical references rather than a single aggregate score.

**Organizer.** The work progressed from learned group supports and actual contextual exchanges to relation-guided original-member admission and E-anchored directories. X retains and uses more nonself E information than S in the measured subset, but shared distant preferences and weak central-region effects leave useful spatial cooperation unresolved. Applied message gates, directory weights, admitted support and final query contributions are different quantities; none alone proves physical causality. The next review should use matched original identities and the three global organization views, plus targeted interventions and numerical work accounting.

**Inverse.** The early spatial work measured finite-change response errors, and later rounds measured model-path and prescribed-control sensitivity. These are informative diagnostics, not validated physical responses or inverse designs. No completed R13-5 through R13-12 inverse campaign is established by the cited reports; a solver-backed response and design evaluation remains future work.

## The resulting HONF model

The central outcome is a short information path. Prescribed geometry, materials, boundaries and controls first enter their owning original M/E records. Two ordered MM→EM→EE→ME contextual cycles update those records and apply learned directed relations. The final E-receiver relations, physical member measures and explicit own-E access prepare an overlapping original-member directory. A query uses adapter-owned geometry to locate an E address, selects the permitted original records before expensive query-conditioned member scores, integrates their contextual values with the inherited typed reader, and produces all physical channels through one general joint-field head. A group is therefore a reusable membership-and-access operation, not a pooled feature that must replace its members.

R13-5 established the value of jointly organized M/E membership and original-member reading. R13-8 removed the expensive global address competition in favor of a clear E service interface. R13-11 made the actual EE relation's width independent of its peak amplitude and exposed a useful access-measure choice. S and X share that core; X changes active-domain measure normalization, rather than introducing another controller or a separate physical-field predictor. Their simplicity is a structural achievement even while full-population comparison, stronger physical responses and inverse-design validation remain open scientific work. The L cover remains a compatible interface option; it is not an additional retained formal candidate in this handoff.

## Evidence identities and reading rules

The principal Thermal full-population comparisons use TRAIN600 and canonical exposed VAL89, with 24/25/25/15 cases in the M3/M5/M7/M10 strata. VAL89 excludes the duplicated 0273 row; it has been repeatedly used for model development and includes source files historically named `test`, so it is not pristine independent TEST evidence. The common TRAIN150/VAL22 input-stratified panel has 6/6/6/4 cases in those strata and is development evidence. Detail scenes and intervention panels can lie outside VAL22; they illustrate fixed cases and do not enlarge its statistical sample. The reports record the exact normalizer and native masks for each lineage; do not equate a TRAIN150-normalized model with a freshly normalized TRAIN600 model or treat their epochs as equal exposure.

Native values below are case-equal errors in packed dataset units, with case p90 and worst values where specified. The module peak is the maintained endpoint maximum error and q is the maintained interface proxy, not a separately validated physical flux. Full access means the model's own legal full reader, which differs across architectures. Joint95 is an admission rule on a joint prior, not a guarantee that 95% of each role's conditional contribution or field accuracy is retained. Best-field selectors and latest states are not interchangeable with literal numbered checkpoints.

This synthesis uses saved reports and their numerical evidence; no model fitting, solver call, evaluation or new physical reference is implied. The dated source reports preserve their original startup and continuation statements: a successful launch is not a completed checkpoint, and a later recorded endpoint supersedes an earlier startup-only statement only for that literal lineage. Historical experiment plans describe intended work, whereas completed reports and receipts establish what actually ran.

## R13-5: joint organization and the consistency supplement

R13-5 forms M and E views from actual staged contextual relations, chooses a joint prefix before nonlinear original-member scoring, and uses typed strengths and a joint predictor. The actual final-cycle relation ledger is $H=\begin{bmatrix}G_{MM}&G_{ME}\\G_{EM}&G_{EE}\end{bmatrix}+\operatorname{diag}(I_M,0_E)$, with receiver-first blocks captured at their respective sequential stages. A query scores both eligible M and E addresses, obtains proposal $\pi_a$, and composes one whole-row-normalized original-member prior $R_{ai}=H_{ai}\mu_i/\sum_jH_{aj}\mu_j$ into $P_i(q)=\sum_a\pi_a(q)R_{ai}$. The shortest joint95 prefix of $P$ is selected before original-member scoring; duplicate member identities are coalesced. The reader computes separate within-role probabilities and learned strengths, integrating contextual original values as $c_t=\rho_t\sum_{i\in t}p_i^tV_i$ into one joint physical head. These operations make a reusable relation govern member access without replacing members by pooled groups; address probabilities, prior mass and final typed contribution are distinct. See the [R13-5 plan](../../UpgradePlan/_bk/20261029_R13_Consolidation/20261027_010044Z_HONF_Round13-5_Joint_ME_Organization_and_Member_Routing_Work_Plan.md) and [R13-6 faithful equation](_bk/20261029_R13_Consolidation/20261027_210114Z_HONF_Round13-6_Faithful_Integration_and_Useful_Organization_Report.md).

## R13-8: E-anchored service and the mature baseline

The E receiver supplies an address and actual incoming EM/EE relations supply its learned membership; structural own-E support is recorded separately from a learned self relation. A selects one geometrically located E service, while L blends the fully computed contexts of a smooth local cover. Both read original contextual M/E records through the same generic joint-field approach. For E anchor $a$, $G_a=[G_{E\leftarrow M},G_{E\leftarrow E}]_a$ captures actual incoming exchanges, and $S_{ai}=\mathbf1_{i=\mathrm{ownE}(a)}$ explicitly grants anchor access. The prepared directory is $R_{ai}=(G_{ai}+S_{ai})\mu_i/\sum_j(G_{aj}+S_{aj})\mu_j$, with one joint M/E normalization and $C_a=\operatorname{Prefix}_{95\%}(R_a)\cup\{\mathrm{ownE}(a)\}$. In each anchor and role, the selected prior weights the calibrated query/member likelihood: $p_{at,i}\propto\mathbf1_{i\in C_a\cap t}R_{ai}e^{\ell_{at,i}(q)}$, $c_{at}=\rho_{at}\sum_i p_{at,i}V_i$. A uses its located anchor; L uses $c_t(q)=\sum_a\lambda_a(q)c_{at}(q)$ and one joint head, blending completed contexts rather than combining priors or predictions. Its union of original members shares raw scores while preserving anchor-specific calibration and reductions. The [R13-8 plan](../../UpgradePlan/_bk/20261029_R13_Consolidation/20261028_022849Z_HONF_Round13-8_E_Anchored_Organization_Read_Work_Plan.md) defines the adapter coverage and actual execution boundary.

## R13-11: stable relations and complementary candidates

The inherited U gate applies a sparse nonlinearity per head before mixture. S instead separates relative width from peak amplitude, C adds a continuous tail, and X uses S with active-domain physical measure for access. Each law acts in the actual two EE contextual cycles; the final applied EM/EE relations form the E-anchored directory. Original records, prescribed inputs, inherited typed calibration and A/L compatibility remain. The [work plan](../../UpgradePlan/_bk/20261029_R13_Consolidation/20261028_225450Z_HONF_Round13-11_Stable_EE_Relations_and_Geometric_Organization_Work_Plan.md) and [completed report](_bk/20261029_R13_Consolidation/20261029_042237Z_HONF_Round13-11_Stable_EE_Relations_and_Geometric_Organization_Report.md) distinguish gate, structural self, directory, admitted support and query contribution.

For each head and E receiver, let $\bar m_i=m_i/\sum_jm_j$, $\bar s=\sum_i\bar m_i s_i$, $v=\sqrt{1+\sum_i\bar m_i(s_i-\bar s)^2}$ and $\delta_i=(\max_j s_j-s_i)/v$ on eligible positive-measure donors. S learns a shared positive width $\tau=\operatorname{softplus}(\theta)$ and applies $G^S_{ai}=\sum_h\omega_h 2\psi(1-\delta_{hai}/\tau)$, with $\psi(x)=\operatorname{ReLU}(x)^2/(1+\operatorname{ReLU}(x)^2)$. The normalized peak is one per head, while width can change independently; this gate executes in contextual exchange and is reused in access. X retains S and replaces the inventory measure in the access directory: for each role's active domain $D_{at}=\{i\in t:G_{ai}+S_{ai}>0\}$, $\mu^{\rm active}_{ai}=\mu_i/\sum_{j\in D_{at}}\mu_j$ and $w_{ai}=(G_{ai}+S_{ai})\mu^{\rm active}_{ai}$, with an empty domain contributing zero. The normalized joint row and prefix then follow the anchored construction. Physical measure is retained within the active domain; this does not impose a role quota or prove whole-model mesh invariance.
