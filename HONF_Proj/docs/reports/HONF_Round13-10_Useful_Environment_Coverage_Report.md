## Equations and faithful execution

For each route, calibrated per-head inputs use eligible donor maxima: `x_hai = gamma*(s_hai - max_j s_haj) + b`. The inherited sparse gate is `G_ai = psi(sum_h omega_h*x_hai)`, where `psi(x) = relu(x)^2/(1+relu(x)^2)`. U uses `G_ai = sum_h omega_h*psi(x_hai)` for EE only. The learned convex head weights and calibration parameters are unchanged; MM, EM and ME retain their previous law. U retains exact zeros when all heads are nonpositive, with no positive floor, degree quota, forced neighbor or target K.

The actual per-head contextual coefficient is reconstructed for diagnosis as `c_hai = A_hai*G_ai/[z_ha + 0.001*(1-z_ha)^2]`, with `A=softmax(s+log(native_measure))` and `z=sum_i A_hai*G_ai`. Saved stage-local reconstruction agrees with the actual snapshot message mass. G is the incidence gate actually applied during ordered exchange and reused in directory construction; c is a normalized contextual coefficient, and neither is a measured physical influence. Same-input support containment for U is an equation property; re-preparing both cycles changes states and does not guarantee whole-scene containment or improved reconstruction.

The inherited directory measure gives each present role unit total: `mu_M=1/N_M`, `mu_E=a_i/sum_E a`. P instead uses `nu_M=1`, `nu_E=N_E*a_i/sum_E a`, counting active positive-measure E records. Both construct `R proportional to (G+S)*measure`, with structural ownE S kept explicit. The compiler retains the shortest positive whole-row prefix reaching coverage tau, with stable role/original-ID ties, and then retains ownE. Empty M remains legal. This changes neither the native physical integration weights nor the original-member predictor. It is a tested access convention, not a correction of an established duplicate-measure bug.

The [anchored core](../../src/honf_forward_core/interaction_reused_member_routing/anchored.py) serializes `access_measure` and `ee_incidence_aggregation`; missing options retain historical behavior. The current anchored path can be written compactly as

$$
 a(q)=\operatorname{locate}_E(q),\qquad G_a=[G_{E\leftarrow M},G_{E\leftarrow E}]_a,\qquad R_{ai}=\frac{(G_{ai}+S_{ai})\mu_i}{\sum_j(G_{aj}+S_{aj})\mu_j},\qquad C_a=\operatorname{Prefix}_{0.95}(R_a)\cup\{a\}.
$$

Here G records the actual applied, stage-specific exchange gates; S is the separately recorded own-E access; the two blocks retain their exchange-stage provenance. The prefix is joint across roles and own-E access is explicitly retained. For each role t, the subsequent query-conditioned distribution is

$$
 p_{i\mid t}(q,a)=\frac{\mathbf1_{i\in C_a\cap t}R_{ai}\exp\ell_i^{a,C}(q)}{\sum_{j\in C_a\cap t}R_{aj}\exp\ell_j^{a,C}(q)},\qquad c_t(q)=\rho_t(q,a)\sum_{i\in C_a\cap t}p_{i\mid t}(q,a)v_i^{\mathrm{ctx}},
$$

Here ell denotes the inherited row/support-dependent calibrated query score, rather than an uncalibrated dot product; rho retains the serialized typed strength based on query, score expectation and normalized entropy. An empty role gives zero context. Member values are prepared once from contextual records; query dependence enters the calibrated scores, probabilities and strength. Original member identity, position and contextual features survive until this read, followed by the joint physical head. The [maintained anchored implementation](../../src/honf_forward_core/interaction_reused_member_routing/anchored.py) therefore exposes the organizational service directly: one row determines eligibility before detailed query–member work, and many queries reuse that row. No all-member value branch outside the directory was added for this review.

U changes the actual EE gate from applying the sparse nonlinearity after mixing heads to mixing the per-head sparse gates. This preserves relationships that different heads support individually and reuses the resulting applied gate in R. The mature evidence locates the remaining loss between relationship formation and execution. Across VAL89 at U1000, raw G has 6.8635 positive nonself E per receiver row on average, and nonself E holds 0.7258% of normalized R mass on average. Yet every one of the 17,088 receiver rows admits zero nonself E at joint95. The population mean structural own-E R share is 1.7536%, with the learned own-E diagonal separately recorded; most access mass lies with M. A joint95 prefix can satisfy its coverage target before admitting a single nonself E. Raising the threshold and expanding contextual support are therefore different operations.

This is narrowing of direct environmental access, not recurrence of the earlier all-zero M/E reader failure. Contextual EE exchange remains live, the query still reads own E and selected M, and the measured paired native backward retains nonzero EE, M-value and E-value gradients. At fixed VAL0678, mean raw nonself-E G degree is 2.8594/11.7917/7.6198 for A500/U500/U1000, whereas all three displayed anchored queries directly read only their own E. The broader contextual states can still improve predictions through those members. ### What R13 has established, and the next consequential refinement

The useful architectural progression is now concrete. A2-D made contextual exchange work; U-H reused applied relationships; R13-5 retained both organization and detailed original-member reading with M/E entrances; R13-8 replaced global entrance competition with a cheap reusable E directory; R13-10 changed the actual EE exchange law rather than attaching a disconnected organizer. The earlier detour through mandatory pooled group functions should not return: query-controlled original-member access is the primary organized read, and a shared summary is an optional operation. The strongest practical R13 result is the combination of that clear access path with large measured modern training and memory savings, while keeping a general joint predictor.

The hypergraph interpretation is operational: each E receiver has a reusable row ledger that separates contextual membership G, structural own-E access S, normalized R and admitted C. Its admitted set serves queries in that E coverage region; some upstream relationships can be omitted from C, and structural self-access need not be a learned interaction. Different rows can share members; a query integrates a permitted set after seeing its own location. The matrix is a representation of those memberships, not the definition of a pooled feature or a claim of physical causality. Attention scores remain pairwise, while the permitted read and its competitive normalization act on a member set. Existing collective interventions help assess set usefulness; the spatial maps above show exactly which relationships survive to execution, rather than treating a visually attractive incidence pattern as sufficient evidence of useful many-body organization.

Two refinements deserve priority over further decoder machinery. Preserve E anchoring, original-member detail and the efficient prepared executor while recovering the historical references' velocity and local material/peak fidelity. In parallel, make relation-to-access calibration express meaningful environmental service after mature learning: the formal U still uses inventory-unit-total measures, so sparse E support multiplied by roughly 1/192 per E competes against much larger per-M measures, and own E is explicitly retained. The observed G→R→C loss identifies that interface as the immediate target, rather than mandating more neighbors, imposing geometric locality or adding another global query scorer. Heterogeneous-environment inputs are the appropriate next stress case for this coverage semantics; Thermal accuracy alone can reward a narrow reader whose E context already contains much of the scene. Any broader-coverage refinement should be evaluated together with retained field/tail fidelity and complete runtime, because the current low cost partly benefits from narrow executed support.

This follow-up adds no inverse-design or physical-solver response experiment. Its completed evidence is native reconstruction, measured execution and directly reconstructed organization/access on saved checkpoints. The numerical and visual evidence is sufficient for a stage conclusion: R13 has produced a simpler, executable organization-controlled neural field; the remaining central issue is preserving useful environmental coverage through mature admission, alongside recovering local physical fidelity. It does not require discarding the anchored architecture or replacing original members with compressed group values.
