## Reader and directory equations

For anchor a, the learned row is the captured final-cycle incoming EM/EE row G. Structural self-access S is recorded separately from the literal applied EE diagonal. The compiler applies the existing role measure once: equal discrete M measure totaling one, and positive physical E measure normalized within E. It normalizes the complete M+E row, compiles one stable joint95 prefix, then includes ownE if absent. There is no M retention quota, role-specific prefix, self-mass floor or target group count.

\[
R_{ai}=\frac{(G_{ai}+S_{ai})\mu_i}{\sum_j(G_{aj}+S_{aj})\mu_j},\qquad C_a=\operatorname{Prefix}_{95\%}(R_a)\cup\{\mathrm{ownE}(a)\}.
\]

The executor expands located packed rows, deduplicates their query/original-member union and evaluates each raw member score once. Calibration, conditional probability, entropy denominator and continuous U read strength remain local to each anchor and role. It coalesces the resulting lambda-times-strength-times-probability coefficients onto original members before gathering values. L combines completed contexts without renormalizing geometric weights over anchors with nonempty M access; one joint trunk/head runs afterward. A/L have no proposal MLP parameters, dense global entrance distribution or H address contractions. U has 1,410,565 trainable elements in 190 parameter tensors; A/L each have 1,376,900 in 186. ### Interpretation and remaining work

Predictor: A/L have not established an across-field reconstruction improvement over original ME500. L gives the better v/p/vorticity/fluid-temperature means of the anchored pair, while A has better surface/material means and lower measured live working allocation. Both retain consequential peak/local-temperature misses, including all validation module-count categories for peaks. Original ME remains the reference to retain when those local observables govern acceptance. The small all-native aggregate deficits do not justify claiming failure of the architecture or convergence, and the earlier U subset comparison cannot settle the mature ME comparison.

Organizer: correct E-ID mapping, positive separately recorded self-access, one joint95 row per prepared scene/E, live learned-relation gradients, query-specific typed strengths and smooth L cover weights are supported by actual execution. The access interventions demonstrate useful nonself information, but not that every learned edge is beneficial or physically causal. Compiled membership compactness, locality and row overlap are descriptive; physical-field utility is tested through the native controls rather than inferred from a prettier graph. The remaining local-fidelity question concerns the combined learned representation, read-strength semantics and generalization under native extraction; full-access controls rule out truncation as the sole cause without isolating a unique alternative cause.

Computation: the measured benefit is substantially lower live allocation and faster complete native/pair-backward execution on the specified panels. The saved work includes actual directory preparation, locator, selected original-member reads, native extraction and checkpoint replay. Reserved cache, data/checkpoint startup, heterogeneous historical wall time and untested workloads remain explicitly separate. A future architectural attribution study would need controlled read/fusion semantics and repeated independent lineages; none was trained, and no controller, quota, auxiliary penalty or alternate model was introduced for this review.

Inverse: no new inverse or solver evidence is added. Forward accuracy, learned support, cost savings and physical/causal interpretation remain separate axes, and exposed VAL89 remains development evidence rather than pristine TEST.
