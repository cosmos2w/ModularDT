## Reader and directory equations

For anchor a, the learned row is the captured final-cycle incoming EM/EE row G. Structural self-access S is recorded separately from the literal applied EE diagonal. The compiler applies the existing role measure once: equal discrete M measure totaling one, and positive physical E measure normalized within E. It normalizes the complete M+E row, compiles one stable joint95 prefix, then includes ownE if absent. There is no M retention quota, role-specific prefix, self-mass floor or target group count.

\[
R_{ai}=\frac{(G_{ai}+S_{ai})\mu_i}{\sum_j(G_{aj}+S_{aj})\mu_j},\qquad C_a=\operatorname{Prefix}_{95\%}(R_a)\cup\{\mathrm{ownE}(a)\}.
\]

The executor expands located packed rows, deduplicates their query/original-member union and evaluates each raw member score once. Calibration, conditional probability, entropy denominator and continuous U read strength remain local to each anchor and role. It coalesces the resulting lambda-times-strength-times-probability coefficients onto original members before gathering values. L combines completed contexts without renormalizing geometric weights over anchors with nonempty M access; one joint trunk/head runs afterward. A/L have no proposal MLP parameters, dense global entrance distribution or H address contractions. U has 1,410,565 trainable elements in 190 parameter tensors; A/L each have 1,376,900 in 186. The difference is exactly the four removed entrance tensors (33,665 elements), with every common initial tensor matched. The [parameter inventory](../../diagnostics/round13_8_20261027/receipts/core_parameter_inventory.json) and executed work counters keep registered parameters separate from runtime use.

The Thermal locator maps physical cell coordinates through prepared E slots, preserving original identities under permutation. A uses half-open containing cells with explicit endpoints.
