# HONF Controlled Maturation and Action-Aware Organization

> **Selected forward weights are frozen; Wind selected inverse sampling is complete and Thermal selected inverse sampling was resource-censored.** Wind G/P are selected at u4910. Thermal G/P are frozen at u1300 as a research-only endpoint: the unchanged runner could not reserve the matched u1301–u1400 block inside the Thermal lane limit. The selected Thermal inverse trained for 32 updates per arm, but two bounded sample-only attempts ended before a condition was complete. The last attempt was stopped when the user requested prompt wrap-up. No selected Thermal terminal sample or forward score exists. Exact attempt and charge records are preserved in the ignored local handoff.

We trained budget-conditioned grouped packet computation (**G**) and an independently fitted direct-pair control (**P**) in WindFarm and ThermalChannel, with full-access replays at the same weights. We then measured root, two-packet and four-packet cuts and fitted an input-only action selector. Wind's grouped hard predictions improved the five-role mean relative to P on the complete development review, but missed individual protected roles; Thermal's selected sparse actions did not pass the physical acceptance rule on any measured primary case. Both fitted selectors chose full-access fallback on all primary cases. This is a measured negative result for deployable sparse organization at these endpoints, while retaining the distinct full-access physical-weight comparisons. The selected-u4910 Wind completion pilot found little difference between forced-root graph and matched dense inverse arms and few row-native acceptable proposals. The selected-u1300 Thermal inverse has a matched train-only checkpoint and CPU interface evidence, but no completed selected-endpoint generation under the bounded attempts. The earlier inverse readiness pilot belongs to an older Stage-C checkpoint.

**Resource scope:** The earlier [upgrade plan](../../../UpgradePlan/ThemalChannel/20260928_HONF_Active_Organization_and_Frozen_Inverse_Reuse_Plan.md) specified 24 GPU-associated hours and shorter primary-update ceilings. Here **G** is the grouped packet model, **P** the direct-pair control, and **B** the retained native full-access reference. ## Decision summary

**Predictor:** Retain Wind u4910 G/P as a bounded research comparison: G-root improves the five-role mean relative to P on both complete 90-row development reviews, but both miss protected individual-role tails. Retain Thermal u1300 only as a resource-censored research endpoint; the saved analytic-wake/shared-grid reference and Run1804 control show physical-role misses even under same-student full access.

**Organizer:** The fixed forward weights produce measured packet and direct-pair access. Wind's case-varying organizer features affect predictions, while the measured Thermal G support mask stayed unchanged across its paired geometry and heat contexts. Both fitted selectors choose full-access fallback on all primary cases. Wind hard execution saves zero measured QE rows and is slower than same-weight full access. Thermal canonical hard work is lower, but native feature rows remain unchanged and full-access cover-executor auxiliary counters are unavailable; its 30-repeat hard wrappers are also slower. These measurements do not establish sparse deployment, executor savings, or physical causal edges.

**Inverse:** Wind's selected-u4910 completion pilot is complete, but forced-root I-G and matched I-dense are bitwise identical on 60/64 as-observed pairs, only 8/64 proposals per arm pass row-native geometry plus stored-surrogate acceptance, and the fitted policy falls back to full access on all 128 replayed terminals. No new-layout CFD validates these proposals. Thermal's selected-u1300 matched training and CPU candidate checks succeeded, but two bounded sample attempts produced zero complete conditions; the second stopped at the user's wrap-up request. The older Run1509 Stage-C readiness pilot remains historical. Neither lane establishes a validated inverse design gain from sparse organization.

The decision keeps fallback separate from sparse success and does not infer valid designs from support size, masked execution, or sampler diversity. No automatic long training continuation follows from this report.
