## Mathematical change and physical axes

Level R separates original physical records from retrieval controls. For N=M+E original records, the native encoder retains its two contextual cycles and their N² pair work. A prepared compact bank directly constructs C∈R^{K×N}; each receiver scores the K live controls, obtains π(q)=entmax(scores(q,K)), and accesses original records through a(q)=π(q)C. The original local M envelope remains in the final M gate. This compresses the retrieval organization while preserving each donor's coordinates, measure, contextual state and query-conditioned nonlinear value. The retained O(QN) information-reader scores are separate from the retired query-to-native-seed scorer.

For thermal outputs, the original source law remains T(q;h)=Σ_i K_i(q)h_i over physically present original M sources. A group centroid is a routing coordinate, never a pooled physical value or a heater. Neither E contraction nor a possible M organizational merge is permitted to replace those physical source columns. Keeping all source columns therefore does not mathematically require the observed no-M-merge policy.

The amendment changes only the backward convention of the pre-cut tie loss. With p=σ(b) and D(p) the discrepancy of the live blended sibling membership rows, the original pD(p) contributes p′D+pD′ to the policy gradient. The amended stopgrad(p)D(p) retains pD′ and removes the explicit positive p′D rejection shortcut; capacity still rewards larger p. Both scalar losses and fixed-weight predictions are unchanged by that convention. Soft descriptor/geometry blending, native objectives and the strict b>0 cut remain live and unchanged. The change enables acceptance; it does not guarantee that accepted pairs are selectively redundant or physically accurate. The maintained implementation is [compact.py](https://github.com/cosmos2w/ModularDT/blob/7981c173e8b1d29ee2b3addb7b972d0f15003542/HONF_Proj/src/honf_forward_core/continuous_joint/compact.py), with direct-bank preparation in [unified.py](../../../../src/honf_forward_core/continuous_joint/unified.py) and original-record access in [model.py](../../../../src/honf_forward_core/continuous_joint/model.py).
