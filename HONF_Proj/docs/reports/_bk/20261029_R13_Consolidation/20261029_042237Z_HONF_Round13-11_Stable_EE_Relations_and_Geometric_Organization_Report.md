## Equations, actual reuse and qualification

For eligible positive-physical-measure donors, each EE head forms `delta=(max(score)-score)/sqrt(1+physical_measure_weighted_variance)` before donor log measure enters contextual coefficients. The inherited convex head mixture produces applied G. Each head peaks at 1, but different head maxima need not coincide, so the mixed row maximum need not equal 1. Empty rows remain empty. No nonself-degree floor, forced neighbor, target K, locality reward or opening schedule is added.
