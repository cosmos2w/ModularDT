# HONF Reference-Anchored Consolidation and Diagnosis — 2026-10-11

This round turns the existing Run2202 direct-to-P bridge into a self-contained mature Wind predictor, adds an optional affine execution path, and measures differences at the real training boundary before changing the learning recipe. The migration retains the historical function and training provenance. Affine factoring preserves the mathematical computation but fails the fixed GPU training and Thermal output criteria, so historical execution remains the default. Results separate matched-age fidelity, learning effects and deployment qualification.

**Predictor:** Mature Wind Run2202 is retained with its full TRAIN420 exposure and e5000 age. Current P is competitive with Run2202 at e1000 on several exposed validation roles, while near-turbine and transverse errors remain limiting. Thermal already contains separate nonlinear flow and affine temperature source reads; its outstanding physical gaps require an optimizer/loss diagnosis rather than an assumed missing read path.

**Organizer:** Saved Thermal P-H results show a specific learning benefit over independently trained P, with higher complete cost and a pressure tradeoff. Wind P-H worsens the preferred near-turbine role despite a small downstream gain. This round does not promote either organizer or infer physical causality from its learned support.

**Inverse:** No inverse campaign, new solver call, or candidate design validation was performed. The solver ledger remains a protected reference at 326/326, and the WindTEST targets remain locked. Saved response evidence retains its original excitation and physical-reference limits. Stored analytic and shared-grid targets do not establish CFD certification.

## Same-function execution and complete cost

The optional executor factors existing source/global/query and MM/ME/EM first-layer projections before their unchanged SiLU nonlinearities. It uses slices of existing learned weights, adds each original bias once, and preserves masks, donor measures, source columns, reductions, native curl and zero-offset affine temperature laws. It adds no learned state and defaults to historical execution. Switching modes invalidates a prepared context. The 64 focused CPU tests cover fields, gradients, JVP/VJP, padding/permutation, reload, native curl and an actual AdamW parity step.

Moments and post-step physical outputs pass their declared checks, but they do not erase the parameter failure. Raw post-step tensor pairs were not saved, so signed elementwise update attribution is unavailable. Thermal inference qualification independently fails all eight P/P-H low/high-M × Q1024/Q8192 cells at fixed 2e-6/2e-5 output tolerances. Normal interface flux fails every cell; omega fails three of four per model, with additional high-M proxy and port-condition failures. Equal masks and small relative norms do not erase these elementwise failures. No tolerance, checkpoint or historical default was changed.

## Organizer decision and physical limits

Saved e2500 DEV22 comparisons show Thermal P-H lowers u/v/omega/fluid-T means by 23.60%/12.07%/22.01%/13.01% versus independent P, while pressure worsens 3.80%. H improves v in 21/22 cases, yet its mean remains 1.70 times contextual D-sep with no D-sep wins. H fluid-T mean 0.529143 also misses P-G's 0.518543. These are a specific dense-model learning benefit under the saved cohort, not a causal decomposition; an independently trained matched-capacity control remains missing.

The current organized path retains every direct source read and adds collective processing. At the new matched GPU2 scopes H costs approximately 1.6 times P at Thermal Q1024 and 1.86–1.91 times at Q8192. Wind H's saved 3% downstream gain accompanies 5% worse near-turbine error, with roughly 1.7–2.0 times the current measured P cost. The earlier two-scene timings used different native scopes and remain separate. No target-free frozen reuse probe was run: this round produced neither a qualified executor gain nor evidence that a legal formation/access/decoding path could meet a physical-error allowance and complete-cost break-even.
