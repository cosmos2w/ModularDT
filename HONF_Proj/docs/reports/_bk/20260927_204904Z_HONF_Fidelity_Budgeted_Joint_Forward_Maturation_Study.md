### Why the earlier weighted organizer loss selected a poor physical tradeoff

The previous G5 predictive stage froze the native predictor and updated only the organizer. The constants and predictive block in `Case_WindFarm/src/windfarm/workflows/native_cover_organizer_fit.py` implement `L = L_teacher + 0.25 L_reference + 0.25 L_label + 0.01 C_work`, with teacher and stored-CFD-target losses in standardized model channels on reused Q1024 search probes, label supervision from train-only G2 plans, and a differentiable logical-work fraction. It imposed no hard physical-reference role budget. The 0.10 teacher gate measured checkpoint-standardized discrepancy, not a 10% allowance against the stored OpenFOAM field.

On the same 24 training rows in predictive cycles 151–174 and 607–630, mean total loss fell `0.054287 → 0.039596` while mean teacher loss rose `0.002869 → 0.004141`, mean native-reference loss rose `0.011771 → 0.014168`, and mean work fraction fell `0.604466 → 0.471834`. Applying the implemented weights to these rounded means gives changes of approximately `+0.001272` teacher, `+0.000599` reference, `−0.015236` label supervision, and `−0.001326` work, summing to the observed `−0.014691` total. The label term supplied most of the favorable weighted change; work pressure also rewarded fewer pairs. This explains how the scalar objective improved despite worse physical and teacher terms. It does not prove that work pressure alone caused the reference regression. The disjoint Q1024 development teacher pass and the two failed complete-grid CFD role guards remain separate evidence.
