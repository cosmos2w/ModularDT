All-open M+E is a different mathematical control from the selected trained function. A same-mask dense-value companion instead tests executor agreement while retaining the trained selected coefficients. Neither comparison may substitute for the other.

All eight full four-epoch engineering cycles have completed: selected and all-open controls for Thermal H/S and Wind H/S.
