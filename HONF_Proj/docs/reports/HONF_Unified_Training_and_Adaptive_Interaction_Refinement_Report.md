# HONF unified training and adaptive interaction refinement: completed formal 5,000-epoch comparison

Updated 9 October 2026 (America/New_York). The latest fresh full-TRAIN fits, **Thermal3904 hard Adaptive and Wind2202 W3 Full-detail**, both completed 5,000 epochs with terminal return code 0. This update adds their literal endpoints to the existing physical comparisons, native field/residual maps, Wind wake cuts, training costs, and saved Thermal response/candidate checks, and now measures both latest Organizers and native GPU inference prices against the preserved controls. Thermal3903 and Wind2201 remain measured historical controls; Thermal1404/1501/Dense 1804/R-direct 3902 and Wind2102 native K6/2103 native dense remain the mature references. All protected histories and the solver ledger are retained.

**Predictor gains and misses:** Thermal3904 lowers mean fluid/surface/material-temperature RMSE by **4.66%/4.81%/2.53% versus 3903**, and has the lowest surface/material/flux-proxy means among the six endpoints. Its mean sampled module-peak error rises **0.51%**, and Dense1804 remains better on fluid temperature: 0.306155 versus 0.237375 makes 3904 **28.98% worse**. Wind2202 improves all 15 component-role means over 2201; near-turbine vector RMSE falls **41.31%**, 0.158383→0.092951 m/s. It beats K6's mean vector RMSE in all five roles, but Dense2103 remains better in all five. These are exposed validation comparisons with different training recipes, not independent TEST results or a causal decomposition of width, queries, balancing and routing.

**Organizer gains and misses:** Latest Thermal3904's learned hard route beats equal-degree nearest/upstream/shuffle and same-weight all-fine on fluid temperature in all three fixed scenes; changing one unprotected read changes temperature and restores exactly. Its selected executor reduces fine rows but remains slower than dense-masked execution, **41.07 versus 38.51 ms**. The full-interface parity limit fails on the flux proxy, so the selected default is retained. Latest Wind2202's learned Full messages also have a measured physical effect, but its optional router is unused and has no optimizer state. Full native inference takes **12.45 ms** versus 2201's 24.40 ms on the same GPU 1 panel; this is a different function and recipe, not adaptive sparsity. [Latest Organizer/cost receipts](../../diagnostics/generated/formal5000_organizer_cost_update_20261009/).

**Inverse gains, misses and next steps:** Thermal3904's mean errors over six signed heating states remain better than R-direct 3902 but are higher than 3903 in every tested role. It again ranks only **2/3 saved candidate pools** correctly: 0291 chooses baseline instead of transfer-plus and incurs **0.390348 native-temperature regret**. Average field gains did not repair this decision. Wind inverse and new-model geometry-response qualification remain untested. The next useful work is Thermal peak/response robustness, Wind transverse fidelity versus Dense 2103, and the flux-proxy numerical qualification of adaptive executors; no new training, optimization campaign or physical solve was performed for this update. WindTEST remains locked and the solver ledger remains **326/326**.

The previous fixed-quarter development results are preserved in the [archived development report](_bk/20261008_113547Z_HONF_Unified_Training_and_Adaptive_Interaction_Refinement_Development_Report.md). Its manifests, inherited-weight experiments, receiver-packet prototype, and bounded pilot results remain development evidence. They are not renamed as formal full-data results. The [previous R-direct report](_bk/20261007_031743Z_HONF_RDirect_Formal_Readiness_and_Receiver_Local_Organization_Report.md) supplies the reporting pattern; its different classic-model selections and old measurements are not imported into this comparison.

## Organizer: what is actually learned and read

The shared implementation remains [SourceRefinement](../../src/honf_forward_core/interface_fields/interaction_refinement.py), [SharedInterfaceContext](../../src/honf_forward_core/interface_fields/common.py) and the [unified TrainingEngine](../../src/honf_runtime/unified_training.py), with separate dataset providers and weights. Thermal3904 deploys `B(q,i) + g(q,i)·[F(q,i)−B(q,i)]` under hard_v1 threshold 0.50/temperature 0.10; every active source retains base influence and all near pairs retain fine detail. It has no forced K or source deletion. Wind2202 deploys Full/all-fine, whose direct fast path returns `F` and **does not execute the base or gate**. Those two laws preserve the existing source-resolved architecture but have different mathematical functions.

The latest checkpoint audit distinguishes learning from merely having a module: all four Thermal3904 router tensors (737 parameters) changed between e100/e5000 and have saved optimizer state at 65,000 steps. All four Wind2202 router tensors (1,025 parameters) are bitwise unchanged between those checkpoints and have **no optimizer state**; its Full loss trains fine/context/readout and auxiliary base approximation, not optional routing. Nine of ten Wind refinement non-router tensors changed. Thresholding that unused router after training is not evidence of learned optional organization. [Thermal training-state audit](../../diagnostics/generated/formal5000_organizer_cost_update_20261009/audit/thermal3904_router_training_audit.json), [Wind training-state audit](../../diagnostics/generated/formal5000_organizer_cost_update_20261009/audit/wind2202_router_training_audit.json).

Thermal's learned context consumes prescribed geometry, source identities, material/inlet/domain inputs and 192 environment records; heat and targets are absent from the context/route inputs and heating is applied afterward. Optional predicted-flow context is disabled. Frozen D-sep 3901 supplies the four flow output channels separately and unchanged. Wind2202 learns 128-dimensional source messages and two dense source/source and source/environment context rounds, then weights messages by physical source measure before its nonlinear velocity head and TRAIN-fitted physical transform. Latent Wind message norms are not additive physical velocity contributions, and learned support is not physical causality.
