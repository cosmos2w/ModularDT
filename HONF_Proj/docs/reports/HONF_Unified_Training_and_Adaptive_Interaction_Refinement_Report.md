# HONF unified training and adaptive interaction refinement: completed formal 5,000-epoch comparison

Updated 9 October 2026 (America/New_York). The latest fresh full-TRAIN fits, **Thermal3904 hard Adaptive and Wind2202 W3 Full-detail**, both completed 5,000 epochs with terminal return code 0. This update adds their literal endpoints to the existing physical comparisons, native field/residual maps, Wind wake cuts, training costs, and saved Thermal response/candidate checks. Thermal3903 and Wind2201 remain measured historical controls; Thermal1404/1501/Dense1804/R-direct3902 and Wind2102 native K6/2103 native dense remain the mature references. All protected histories and the solver ledger are retained.

**Predictor gains and misses:** Thermal3904 lowers mean fluid/surface/material-temperature RMSE by **4.66%/4.81%/2.53% versus 3903**, and has the lowest surface/material/flux-proxy means among the six endpoints. Its mean sampled module-peak error rises **0.51%**, and Dense1804 remains better on fluid temperature: 0.306155 versus 0.237375 makes 3904 **28.98% worse**. Wind2202 improves all 15 component-role means over 2201; near-turbine vector RMSE falls **41.31%**, 0.158383→0.092951 m/s. It beats K6's mean vector RMSE in all five roles, but Dense2103 remains better in all five. These are exposed validation comparisons with different training recipes, not independent TEST results or a causal decomposition of width, queries, balancing and routing.

**Organizer gains and misses:** The preserved 3903/2201 and bounded W3 Adaptive interventions verify actual readout effects, retained base paths and exact restoration. Their selected executors did not deliver useful same-function wall-clock savings. **Wind2202 is Full-detail/all-fine**, a different mathematical function from Adaptive; its fidelity gain is not a sparsity result. Thermal3904 retains the established hard gate; the inconclusive C1 transfer was not promoted. New checkpoint inference is measured here on CPU; historical GPU call prices and routing-control results remain explicitly attributed to their original weights.

**Inverse gains, misses and next steps:** Thermal3904's mean errors over six signed heating states remain better than R-direct3902 but are higher than 3903 in every tested role. It again ranks only **2/3 saved candidate pools** correctly: 0291 chooses baseline instead of transfer-plus and incurs **0.390348 native-temperature regret**. Average field gains did not repair this decision. Wind inverse and new-model geometry-response qualification remain untested. The next useful work is Thermal peak/response robustness, Wind transverse fidelity versus Dense2103, and adaptive executor cost; no new training, optimization campaign or physical solve was performed for this update. WindTEST remains locked and the solver ledger remains **326/326**.

The previous fixed-quarter development results are preserved in the [archived development report](_bk/20261008_113547Z_HONF_Unified_Training_and_Adaptive_Interaction_Refinement_Development_Report.md). Its manifests, inherited-weight experiments, receiver-packet prototype, and bounded pilot results remain development evidence. They are not renamed as formal full-data results. The [previous R-direct report](_bk/20261007_031743Z_HONF_RDirect_Formal_Readiness_and_Receiver_Local_Organization_Report.md) supplies the reporting pattern; its different classic-model selections and old measurements are not imported into this comparison.

The latest runs freshly initialized weights, optimizer states and full-TRAIN transforms, rather than continuing pilot/development checkpoints. Wind2202 uses selected W3 Full-detail H128/message128/E8, TRAIN-fitted component balancing, Q4096, effective/microbatch 24 and query tile 512. Thermal3904 uses the established hard-gated source refinement, Q1024, effective/microbatch 48, material 32, surface stride 4 and 128 operator rows/case, with frozen 3901 flow. Both use packing-independent `case_epoch_v1` streams. Thermal micro 48 preserves physical query IDs and effective 48, but its existing union-padding base loss differs 1.66–5.23% from micro 8 in measured preflight; reconstruction/operator/response and aggregate gradient differences were near 1e−7. This disclosed auxiliary change, the prior micro-index-dependent sampling and the new monitoring cadence prevent a single-factor speed/fidelity attribution. The 5000 schedule has fine warmup through 500, opening through 600, soft routing through 800 and deployed inference from 801; Wind's deployed mode is all-fine Full, Thermal's is hard Adaptive. Fine LR holds through 2000 then decays. Initial completed-epoch output, 100-epoch histories/curves/latest states, declared milestones and best-field aliases are saved. Terminal progress, fit summaries and actual optimizer payloads establish completion.

## Organizer: what is actually learned and read

The shared implementation is actual source refinement in [SourceRefinement](../../src/honf_forward_core/interface_fields/interaction_refinement.py), [SharedInterfaceContext](../../src/honf_forward_core/interface_fields/common.py), and the [unified TrainingEngine](../../src/honf_runtime/unified_training.py), with separate dataset providers and separate learned weights. At a source–receiver pair, detail is `B(q,i) + g(q,i)·[F(q,i)−B(q,i)]`; all active physical sources retain their cheap base path and protected near pairs retain fine detail. Historical 3903/2201 and latest Thermal3904 use hard routing at threshold 0.50 and temperature 0.10. Latest Wind2202 explicitly uses Full-detail/all-fine; changing to Adaptive would change the mathematical function. Production routing imposes no forced K, source deletion, or latent-bank revival. Equal-degree nearest/upstream/shuffle routes are diagnostic interventions only.

Thermal prepares a heat-independent kernel and applies the heating controls affinely, preserving source order and precise FP64 native accumulation/increments. Run3903’s formal thermal context uses prescribed geometry/material/inlet/domain inputs; optional predicted-flow context is **disabled**, and target flow is never an input. Frozen D-sep3901 supplies the reported flow outputs separately. Wind2201 retains individual 64-dimensional source messages; latest 2202 uses 128-dimensional messages and physical source measures, then applies a nonlinear velocity readout. A Wind latent-message norm is not an additive physical velocity contribution. These contracts share executable interaction code while preserving the distinct affine and nonlinear output laws.
