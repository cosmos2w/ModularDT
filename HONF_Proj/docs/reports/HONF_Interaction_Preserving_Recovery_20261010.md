# HONF Interaction-Preserving Joint Core Recovery: Primary Segmented Result and Open Follow-up

**Status:** the original six-arm P/P-G/P-H segmented lineage completed 2,500 epochs in both tasks, and the frozen field-only selector chose Thermal P/P-G at epoch 2,400 and P-H at 2,500, plus Wind P/P-G/P-H at 2,500; fixed-scope native fields, same-DEV graph interventions, and the extracted same-weight Wind direct-cost audit are measured, while the plan’s sole Thermal curl-consistency follow-up has been applied and is awaiting calibration and matched fits.

The experiment restored a nonlinear source-conditioned MM/ME/EM direct path before adding one shallow collective residual, kept Thermal flow and temperature in one jointly trained model, and retained Wind’s shared nonlinear core; fresh P is the needed direct source-conditioned control, whereas J-direct is pooled context. Thermal P-H improves mean v, omega, and temperature error over P by about 23.5%, 28.9%, and 15.5%, but still misses the D-sep transverse-flow floor and costs 1.93× P, so the organizer is not promoted as a recovery solution. Wind P is positive against W2302 J-geometry on the matched 24-row role panel, while P-H remains a mixed accuracy/overhead candidate, and the full-data mature Run2202 direct model remains unequal-exposure context rather than a matched source-resolved control.
