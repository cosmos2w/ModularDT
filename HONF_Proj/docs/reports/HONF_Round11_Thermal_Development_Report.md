### Revised interpretation and retained limits

For prediction, S is now the strongest balanced mean-error checkpoint in this measured full-data U triplet, while H remains the fluid-temperature/vorticity specialist. H's more selective second contextual cycle and group-composed retrieval coexist with its temperature gain, but S's stronger means on seven quantities show that more explicit grouping is not a universal accuracy advantage. The different fitted trajectories and classic normalization/objectives prevent attributing those gains causally to grouping alone. The original e 5000 development tables remain valid for their older checkpoints and panels; they should no longer serve as the sole ranking of the new full-data runs. Existing training horizons and checkpoints are preserved rather than changed by this review.

For organization, retain the applied native-record construction and the measured composition checks, while treating hyperedge count, exact-zero support, final value work and physical interpretation as separate claims. The absence of a one-group collapse and the stronger later EE selection are useful structural evidence; physical group validity and causal relevance are not established by these heatmaps. For inverse use, no response/candidate replay was performed with these new epoch 1000 weights, so the improved field metrics do not establish design ranking, geometry transfer or inverse feasibility. The earlier inverse limitations below remain historical evidence, not a validation of the new models. No optional E-access trial, new router, optimizer step, solver call or unexposed-TEST read was introduced.
