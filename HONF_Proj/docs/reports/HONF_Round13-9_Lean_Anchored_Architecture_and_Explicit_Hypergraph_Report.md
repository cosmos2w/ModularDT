## Preserved equations and measured simplifications

For each original E receiver, the directory uses actual applied final-cycle incoming EM and EE relations G and a separately declared structural ownE incidence S. Its joint access measure is `R_i ∝ (G_i + S_i) μ_i`, with equal discrete M measure totaling one and positive physical E-cell measure normalized within E. This access measure is separate from native physical integration measures. The shortest positive-weight whole-row prefix reaching τ is admitted with existing role/original-ID tie behavior, then ownE is retained. The measured attained mass can exceed τ. There is no separate per-role coverage target, forced M winner, forced locality or target K.

Within each admitted role, `p_i(q) ∝ R_i exp(ell_i(q))`; each completed typed context is `rho_t(q) Σ_i p_i(q) V_i`. A uses one geometrically located E receiver. L computes calibration, normalization and strength separately within every covered row, then blends completed contexts with its smooth locator weights. The generic joint head receives q and both typed contexts. Original identity, position, physical measure and contextual value persist until query-dependent reading. R controls joint admission, p controls within-role composition, and rho controls typed amplitude; the role share in R is not silently multiplied into amplitude a second time. Applied EM/EE stages are ordered exchanges, not a simultaneous physical influence matrix.
